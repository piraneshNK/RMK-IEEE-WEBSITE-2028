Official Government Tourism Portal

Chennai District | Tamil Nadu Tourism

Visit Chennai District Portal

As the capital city of Tamil Nadu (formerly known as Madras), Chennai serves as the cultural, historical, and economic gateway to South India. Situated along the Coromandel Coast off the Bay of Bengal, the city seamlessly blends a 400-year-old colonial heritage and ancient Dravidian temple traditions with a vibrant cosmopolitan lifestyle, world-renowned Carnatic music and Bharatanatyam arts, and thriving technology and educational hubs.

The official Chennai District – Tamil Nadu Tourism guide provides conference delegates with comprehensive visitor information, curated sightseeing itineraries, heritage trails, cultural insights, and travel planning resources across the district.

Beaches & Coastal Promenade

Home to the iconic Marina Beach—the second-longest urban beach in the world stretching 13 km—and the serene shores of Elliot's Beach (Besant Nagar), offering scenic sunrises, historic statues, and lighthouse views.

Heritage & Spiritual Landmarks

Explore ancient architectural marvels including the 7th-century Kapaleeshwarar Temple in Mylapore, the 8th-century Pallava-built Parthasarathy Temple in Triplicane, and the Neo-Gothic San Thome Cathedral Basilica.

Forts, Museums & Monuments

Visit Fort St. George (built in 1639), the historic Government Museum at Egmore (established in 1851, renowned for Chola bronzes), and the chariot-shaped Valluvar Kottam memorial dedicated to classical Tamil poet Thiruvalluvar.

Nature, Culture & Cuisine

Experience urban biodiversity at Guindy National Park, traditional South Indian performing arts at Kalakshetra, iconic handloom Kanchipuram silks, and authentic Tamil culinary hospitality.
