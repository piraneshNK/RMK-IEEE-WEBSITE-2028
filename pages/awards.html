PHOTONICS AI 2028

Conference Awards

Excellence & Recognition Program

Celebrating research breakthroughs, oral delivery, and service leadership

The PHOTONICS AI 2028 committee will select the following Awards during the conference to honor scientific contributions of the highest quality, exceptional presentations, and outstanding community service.

01 Paper Awards
Young Scientist

Outstanding Young Scientist Award

Recognizes outstanding contributions by young scientists. Applicants must be born after 1 January 1989 and have an accepted paper with completed registration.

Category No. 01 Born after Jan 1, 1989
Top Honor

Best Paper Award

Awarded to the paper with the highest technical quality, originality, and presentation among all accepted and registered papers.

Category No. 02 All Accepted Papers
Student Only

Best Student Paper Award

Awarded to the best paper whose first author is a full-time student with valid enrollment at the time of submission.

Category No. 03 Full-Time Students
Innovation

Best Innovation Research Award

Recognizes papers that introduce breakthrough ideas and novel methodologies in power and energy research.

Category No. 04 Novel Methodologies
02 Presentation & Service Awards
Oral Session

Best Oral Presentation Award

Selected from all oral presentations based on delivery, clarity, and engagement throughout the technical tracks.

Session Award Oral Presenters
Reviewer Service

Outstanding Reviewer Award

Awarded to reviewers who have provided thorough, timely, and insightful reviews in maintaining conference proceedings quality.

Service Recognition Peer Reviewers
Volunteer Service

Outstanding Volunteer Award

Awarded to volunteers who have demonstrated exceptional dedication and support throughout the planning and execution of PHOTONICS AI 2028.

Community Honor Conference Volunteers
