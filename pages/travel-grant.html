About the Travel Grant

IEEE PHOTONICS AI 2028 invites students, researchers, and faculty members working in the area of PHOTONICS to apply for the Travel Grant award Program.
The Travel Grant includes reimbursement (fixed amount) towards travel for attending the conference. Travel Grants are open to Indian students, researchers, and faculty actively engaged in PHOTONICS domains.
Travel Grant recipients are required to attend the entire two-day conference, and attendance will be formally recorded. The travel grant is intended as a facilitative support mechanism for participants who may otherwise lack adequate institutional funding, and should not be viewed as an honor or award.
Applicants must be full-time students or regular faculty members at the time of the conference. College identity cards and Aadhaar cards will be verified during registration.
The selection and acceptance of travel grant applications will be solely based on the criteria defined by the IEEE PHOTONICS AI Travel Grant Committee.

Travel Grant Guidelines

The applicant must be a registered full-time student (UG/PG/Ph.D.) or a regular faculty member of a recognized institution, actively engaged in the area of Photonics or allied domains.
A Bonafide Certificate (Template Provided below) issued by the Head of the Department confirming full-time status, along with a valid institutional identity card and Aadhaar card, must be produced at the time of claiming reimbursements.
All travel grant recipients are required to attend all tutorials and technical sessions for the entire duration of the conference.
Attendance is mandatory on all days of the conference, and fellows are expected to report to the conference venue by 9:00 AM each day and remain until the conclusion of all scheduled technical sessions.
Travel grant awardees who are authors of regular papers or participants in the Academia–Research Track are also required to strictly comply with these guidelines.
Applicants availing travel support must undertake their journey to and from the conference from their official place of work to qualify for reimbursement. In cases where travel originates from the applicant’s residence, valid proof of residence must be submitted for consideration.
Attendance & Forfeiture Clause
Failure to maintain mandatory attendance will result in the forfeiture of reimbursement of travel expenses. Late arrivals or early departures will not be permitted; in such cases, the Fellowship Committee reserves the right to partially or fully forfeit the reimbursement amount.

Selection Criteria & Requirements

Accepted Papers: Authors of accepted papers will be given priority consideration.
Academic Merit: Evaluation is based on academic achievements and research contributions in photonics.
Statement of Purpose: Convincing Statement of Purpose for attending IEEE PHOTONICS AI 2028.
Faculty Recommendation: Faculty applicants must upload a recommendation letter from their Head of the Department/Principal/Director on official institutional letterhead.
Student Recommendation: Student applicants are required to submit a recommendation letter from their Supervisor or Head of the Department on official institute/department letterhead.
Applications submitted after the stipulated deadline will not be considered. Shortlisted candidates will be informed via email regarding subsequent stages.
