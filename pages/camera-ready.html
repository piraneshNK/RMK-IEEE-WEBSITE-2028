Camera-Ready Paper Preparation

Submission Notice

Camera-ready paper preparation is currently unavailable. Please proceed only after receiving the acceptance notification.

Authors of accepted papers have to submit their camera-ready paper as an IEEE Xplore-compliant PDF. The specific operation time and process shall be subject to the notice of the conference committee. Authors can read the following instructions carefully in advance.

Step 01

Before Creating Your PDF File

Author templates & initial preparation

Use the official IEEE conference templates (MS Word or LaTeX) to create your source document. Proofread your document thoroughly to confirm that it requires no revision before PDF generation.

Microsoft Word
IEEE Letter Template (.docx)
LaTeX Archive
LaTeX Template Package (.zip)
Copyright Notice Placement
Add the copyright notice to the bottom of the first page of your source document.
Copyright notice text: To be provided by the conference committee.
Step 02

Create Your PDF eXpress Account

IEEE Xplore PDF compliance & certification

1
Log in to IEEE PDF eXpress. (Site opening dates: TBA; closing date: TBA).
2
First-time users: Click “Create account”; enter the conference ID (to be determined), your email address, and a password; complete the prompts. An online and email confirmation will verify account setup.
3
Previous users: Users of PDF eXpress or IEEE PDF eXpress Plus should follow the same steps using the password used for previous conferences. Verify that your contact information is valid.
4
Upload your file, convert it, and check the conversion status report. Confirm whether conversion succeeded or changes are needed.
Contacting PDF eXpress Support
Access the Publications Support Center for IEEE PDF eXpress. If you do not find an answer in the Find Answers tab, navigate to the “Ask A Question” tab for direct technical assistance.
Step 03

Paper Upload

Final certified manuscript dispatch

After careful verification and receipt of your IEEE-compliant certified PDF, upload the final manuscript to the conference email:

subash.ece@rmkec.ac.in
Click to email final PDF manuscript
Step 04

IEEE Electronic Copyright Form (eCF)

One form per paper, not per author

In order to be published in the conference proceedings, all accepted papers MUST be accompanied by a signed IEEE Copyright Form.

1
Log in to the CMT Submission System.
2
Click the ‘Submit IEEE Copyright Form’ link from the Author Console and enter required information as prompted.
3
After completing the IEEE copyright form on the eCF site, download the signed copy.
4
Upload the form into CMT and forward a copy to conference email: subash.ece@rmkec.ac.in.
