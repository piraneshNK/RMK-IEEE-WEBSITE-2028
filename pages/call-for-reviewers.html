PHOTONICS AI 2028

Call for Reviewers

Expert Peer-Review Community

Ensuring academic rigor, scientific validity, and publication excellence

PHOTONICS AI 2028 will have approximately 300–350 reviewers (including TPC members and additional experts) to ensure the quality of the conference proceedings. We sincerely welcome experts in the areas of intelligent photonic systems to join the conference as reviewers.

Reviewers evaluate article submissions based on the requirements of the conference proceedings, predefined criteria, and the quality, completeness, and accuracy of the research presented. They suggest improvements and make a recommendation about whether to accept, reject, or request changes to the article.

300–350 Expert Reviewers
3-Day Fast Turnaround
Double-Blind Review

Qualifications of Reviewers

To ensure the high academic quality of PHOTONICS AI 2028 proceedings, applicants are expected to meet these qualifications:

Academic Degree: Hold a Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Computer Engineering, or a closely related field.
Publication Record: Have published at least 2–3 peer-reviewed papers in reputable international journals or conferences within the recent 5 years.
Domain Expertise: Possess demonstrable expertise in specific technical areas relevant to the conference topics (Photonic Integrated Circuits, Optical Neural Networks, Silicon Photonics, AI Acceleration, etc.).
Professional Role: Currently hold a faculty or research position (e.g., Professor, Associate Professor, Senior Researcher, or Postdoctoral Fellow) or an equivalent senior engineering role in industry.
Peer-Review Experience: Previous experience in journal or conference peer review is highly preferred.

How to Apply as a Reviewer

Please send your short CV and list of publications to subash.ece@rmkec.ac.in. Your application will be processed within 3 working days.
