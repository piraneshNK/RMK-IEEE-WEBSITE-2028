Attractions & Sightseeing

DISCOVER CHENNAI & TAMIL NADU

Explore Attractions Around the Venue

Make the most of your visit to IEEE Photonics AI 2028. From UNESCO World Heritage monuments to scenic coastlines and ancient Dravidian temples, explore the cultural richness of Chennai and Tamil Nadu.

Nature & Wildlife ~25 km from Venue

Pulicat Lake & Bird Sanctuary

The second largest brackish water lake in India, located close to Kavaraipettai. Renowned for hosting thousands of migratory flamingos, pelicans, and scenic boat rides alongside a historic 17th-century Dutch cemetery.

Highlight: Flamingo Bird Watching Nearest to Campus
UNESCO World Heritage ~95 km from Venue

Group of Monuments at Mahabalipuram

Famous 7th-century Pallava dynasty rock sanctuaries overlooking the Bay of Bengal. Features the iconic Shore Temple, Pancha Rathas (Five Chariots), and the colossal open-air bas-relief "Descent of the Ganges".

Highlight: Shore Temple & Stone Carvings Day Trip Recommended
Iconic City Landmark ~45 km from Venue

Marina Beach Promenade

Stretching for 13 km along the Coromandel Coast, Marina Beach is the second longest natural urban beach in the world. Ideal for evening strolls, lighthouse views, and vibrant local coastal street food culture.

Highlight: Sunset Views & Lighthouse Central Chennai
Heritage & Architecture ~46 km from Venue

Kapaleeshwarar Temple, Mylapore

A masterwork of 7th-century Dravidian architecture dedicated to Lord Shiva. Features a towering 37-metre gopuram (gateway tower) adorned with intricate multi-hued mythological sculptures and a serene temple tank.

Highlight: Dravidian Gopuram & Tank Mylapore Heritage
Living History Museum ~70 km from Venue

DakshinaChitra Living Museum

An open-air living-history museum along the East Coast Road (ECR) showcasing 18 authentic heritage houses from Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh, complete with live craft demonstrations and folk performances.

Highlight: South Indian Folk Art & Craft East Coast Road
Historic Basilica ~47 km from Venue

San Thome Cathedral Basilica

One of only three known basilicas in the world built directly over the tomb of an apostle of Jesus Christ (St. Thomas). Features stunning Neo-Gothic architecture, stained glass windows, and a museum of antiquities.

Highlight: Neo-Gothic Spires & Tomb Santhome, Chennai

Local Travel & Excursion Advice for Conference Attendees

The conference venue at R.M.K. Engineering College (Kavaraipettai) is directly connected to Chennai Central Station via frequent suburban EMU trains (~1 hour transit) and along National Highway 16 (NH 16). App-based cabs (Uber, Ola) and pre-arranged campus shuttles will be available during conference days. For post-conference heritage excursions to Mahabalipuram and DakshinaChitra, half-day and full-day air-conditioned taxi hires can be arranged via the conference hospitality desk.
